# Workforce Intelligence — Live Hackathon Demo Walkthrough (3 Minutes)

This script provides a structured, high-impact demonstration flow designed for hackathon judges evaluating **Matching Accuracy**, **Team Optimization**, **Skill Gap Diagnostics**, and **Workload Balancing**.

---

## ⏱️ Act 1: The Problem & Scenario A — AI Team Matching (90 Seconds)

### 1. Opening Hook (10s)
> *"Welcome to Workforce Intelligence. In modern engineering organizations, staffing strategic projects is either guesswork or spreadsheets. Either seniors burn out, or projects fail because of hidden skill deficits. Here is how autonomous intelligence solves both."*

### 2. Enter Scenario A (Hero Screen) (30s)
1. **Point to the Project Header:**
   - Note the project: **"Enterprise Analytics Engine"** (Critical Priority, 4 Headcount needed, Deadline Dec 15, 2026).
   - Point to the requirements: **Python (L4, 5x)**, **Cloud Architecture (L4, 5x)**, **Data Analytics (L4, 4x)**, **SQL (L3, 3x)**.
2. **Highlight the Team Capability Coverage Bar (68%):**
   - Point out the radial score ring: *"At a glance—under 5 seconds—any judge can see the team is currently deficient at 68% coverage."*
   - Show the skill breakdown: *"Python is 100%, SQL is 100%, but Cloud Architecture is only at 60% with an alert: Skill Deficit Detected."*

### 3. Interactive Member Swap & Live Recomputation (30s)
1. Look at candidate card **Sophia Mueller** (Junior Data Scientist, 62% fit score).
2. Click **"Swap Member"**:
   - The swap modal slides open with ranked alternatives.
   - Choose **Chloe Zhang** or **Marcus Chen** and click **"Swap In"**.
3. **Observe the Live Reaction:**
   - Watch the radial ring spring to **100% Optimal Alignment**!
   - The Cloud Architecture progress bar fills to 100% green.

### 4. Direct Upskilling Link (20s)
1. Point to the yellow warning banner: *"Even before swapping, the platform automatically synthesizes an upskilling pathway."*
2. Click **"View Courses"** on the banner:
   - Smoothly jumps to the **Upskill & Training** screen.
   - Highlights the course: **"Advanced AWS Multi-Region Cloud Architecture & Serverless"**, showing enrolled team members and expected resolution timeline.

## ⏱️ Act 2: Scenario B — Burnout Mitigation & Workload Rebalancing (60 Seconds)

### 1. Navigate to "Workload & Balance" (10s)
1. Click **"Workload & Balance"** in the sidebar.
2. Point out the Executive KPI summary:
   - **3 Overloaded staff (>100% in Red):** Marcus Chen (130%), Elena Rostova (120%), David Okafor (115%).
   - **4 Underutilized staff (<50% in Dark Magenta):** Chloe Zhang (35%), Tariq Mansour (40%), Sophia Mueller (37.5%), Lucas Silva (45%).

### 2. Live Interactive Rebalancing (30s)
1. Scroll to the **"Smart Workload Rebalancer"** engine.
2. Review Proposal #1:
   - **From:** Marcus Chen (Senior Cloud Architect, 52h/wk).
   - **To:** Chloe Zhang (Cloud Associate, AWS Certified, 14h/wk).
   - **Action:** Transfer 12 hours of K8s Cluster Monitoring.
3. Click **"Accept Shift"**:
   - Notice the before/after status counter instantly update: *"2 Overloaded (1 Shift Accepted)"*.
   - Look at Marcus Chen's card below: his red utilization bar **springs smoothly down to 40h (100% healthy green)**!
   - Look at Chloe Zhang's card: her dark magenta bar **springs up to 26h (65% healthy green)**!
4. Show **Undo Flexibility**:
   - Click **"Undo"** on the proposal: watch the bars spring back to baseline.
   - Re-click **"Accept Shift"** to show instant reactivity.

### 3. Accept Proposals #2 & #3 (20s)
1. Accept Elena Rostova -> Tariq Mansour (10h SQL pipeline).
2. Accept David Okafor -> Lucas Silva (8h React table).
3. **Closing Punchline:**
   > *"In three clicks, we transitioned our top three leads out of burnout risk, mobilized 30 hours of latent capacity, and kept all projects fully on schedule—with complete visibility and zero guesswork."*

1. Point to the **Firebase Status indicator** in the sidebar footer.
2. Highlight the architecture:
