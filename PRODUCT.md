# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Engineering Directors, Resource Managers, Staffing Leads, and HackAttack Hackathon Judges evaluating workforce utilization, skill gap diagnostics, and team composition efficiency.

## Product Purpose

Workforce Intelligence matches talent to strategic projects by balancing skill proficiencies, domain experience, and live availability. It automatically identifies capability deficits, recommends targeted training curricula, detects burnout risks, and enables single-click workload rebalancing.

## Brand Personality

Restrained, tactile, authoritative, fluid. Inspired by Apple WWDC design principles with translucent system materials, precision typography, and spring-based physical motion.

## Anti-references

Generic AI dashboard slop, loud multi-colored gradient blobs, emoji iconography, flat un-styled tables, bloated nested cards, and static unresponsive interfaces.

## Design Principles

- **5-Second Insight:** Every metric, match score, and gap diagnostic must be immediately legible and interpretable.
- **Physical Fluidity:** Every transition uses physics-based springs (damping 1.0 default, ~0.8 for momentum release) with zero input latency.
- **Semantic Color Restraint:** Single Apple Blue accent (`#0071E3` / `#2997FF`); status colors (red, yellow, green, cyan) reserved exclusively for operational states.
- **Agency and Reversibility:** Critical operational actions (member swaps, task rebalancing) provide live before/after diffs with individual undo capabilities.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA compliance, accessible ARIA roles and labels on all progress rings and charts, keyboard navigation, focus indicators, and full support for `prefers-reduced-motion`, `prefers-reduced-transparency`, and `prefers-contrast`.
