## 2. Mandatory Rules for Frontend Work

Whenever executing any frontend work:
1. **Never build generic, boring MVPs**: Combine Category B (High-End Aesthetics) with Category A (Apple Fluid Motion) to make interfaces feel tactile, intentional, and premium.
2. **Apply Fluid Physics by Default**: Use spring physics (`damping: 1.0` default; `~0.8` for momentum), pointer-down instant responsiveness, interruptible animations, and translucent glass layers (`backdrop-filter`).
3. **Honor Typography & Optical Hierarchy**: Tighten tracking on large display headings (`-0.02em`), relax body leading, and build hierarchy using weight and size in harmony.
4. **Cover All 5 Interaction States**: Never leave loading, empty, error, or disabled states unstyled.
5. **Always Respect Accessibility**: Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`, `prefers-reduced-transparency`, and maintain strong contrast standards.
